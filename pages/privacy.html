PRIVACY

Your information, your control.

This project privacy page describes the information TechNova may process.

Account data

Account information is handled through the Supabase authentication service configured for this project. Profile information may include your display name and bio.

Event registrations

Registrations made through the TechNova account system store the selected event and your account identifier so the registration can appear on your account page.

Contact messages

Messages submitted through the contact page are stored in the TechNova database so they can be reviewed and handled.

Browser preferences

Some optional interface preferences may be stored locally in your browser.

Managing your information

Use Account settings to update your profile or contact TechNova about questions regarding the project.
