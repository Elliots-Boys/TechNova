FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

Questions, answered.

Quick answers about the TechNova website.

Do I need an account to browse events?

No. You can browse the public event list without signing in. Registration can be completed through the event registration form.

What does my account do?

Your account stores your profile, password and registrations made through TechNova's account system.

Can I remove an event registration?

Yes. Open My account and use the Remove button beside a registration you no longer want.

How do I change my name or bio?

Open Account settings from your account dashboard, edit the profile section and save it.

How do I contact TechNova?

Use the Contact page. Messages are saved securely for the site's contact workflow.

Is TechNova a real company?

TechNova is the technology platform project you're building. The company and contact details shown on the site are project content.
